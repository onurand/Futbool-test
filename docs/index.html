Direct view
United are a clear but not crushing home favourite. Market pricing the home win around 55% fair probability. If there's a surprise tonight, the draw is more likely than a Leeds win.
Market read
Across 12 bookmakers (snapshot 17:02 UTC): home mean 1.73, draw 3.87, away 4.95. Vig-clean fair probabilities: 55.1% / 24.5% / 20.4%.
Why the market leans this way
United have won 2 of their last 5 at Old Trafford with a 1.8 goals-for average. Leeds are light away (0W-1D-1L last two) and leak goals (1.6 xG conceded). Bamford doubtful on the Leeds side trims their ceiling further.
My angle
I'm with the market on the base case. United's price looks fairly struck — I wouldn't pay shorter than 1.70. Slight lean is that the draw is under-priced if United struggle to break a deep block.
If there is a surprise
The cleanest upset form is the draw, not a Leeds win. United's recent low-block struggles tilt the surprise toward 1-1 or 2-2, rather than an outright away win which the market (correctly) treats as a longshot.
